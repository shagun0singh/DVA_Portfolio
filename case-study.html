DVA-Focused Portfolio Case Study

Prepared by SHAGUN SINGH

This case study reframes my DVA capstone work into a portfolio-ready business story with a clear problem, measurable KPIs, actionable insights, and decision-focused recommendations.

1) Problem Statement and Stakeholder Context

The business wanted to understand what drives customer behavior and performance variation across restaurants so teams can improve engagement, satisfaction, and growth.

  • Primary stakeholders: Operations team, marketing team, and leadership.
  • Key decisions: Which factors influence ratings, pricing response, and customer activity.

2) Dataset Source and Scope

Source dataset: Zomato capstone dataset from the DVA coursework repository.

  • Scope includes restaurant metadata, location-level attributes, pricing, and ratings.
  • Analysis focuses on identifying business patterns and customer-facing performance metrics.

3) Cleaning and Transformation Summary

  • Handled missing values and standardized inconsistent category labels.
  • Normalized text fields and validated numeric ranges for quality checks.
  • Created analysis-ready fields for segmentation and dashboard filtering.

4) KPI Framework

  • Average rating by segment/location
  • Distribution of restaurants by price range
  • Customer engagement indicators by category
  • Top and bottom performing segments

5) Key Insights (3-5)

  • Customer ratings vary significantly across location and pricing tiers.
  • Mid-range pricing bands tend to show stronger consistency in ratings.
  • Some high-volume segments underperform on quality metrics, signaling operational gaps.
  • Segment-level targeting can improve outcomes faster than one-size-fits-all campaigns.

7) Recommendations and Expected Impact

  • Prioritize low-performing high-volume segments for immediate process improvements.
  • Use pricing and location insights to optimize promotions and campaign targeting.
  • Track KPI movement monthly to validate impact and iterate decisions quickly.
